---
description: Explain anything clearly, step by step, in plain language
---

Explain the topic clearly and patiently, as if teaching someone new to the field.

- Start with a one-sentence plain-language summary of the key idea.
- Build up in small steps; introduce one concept at a time and connect it to the last.
- Use a short analogy or a concrete example to make it tangible.
- Name any jargon the first time it appears and immediately define it in plain terms.
- End with a 2-3 bullet recap and one small thing to try next.

Keep the tone warm and encouraging. Avoid walls of text; prefer short paragraphs and lists.
