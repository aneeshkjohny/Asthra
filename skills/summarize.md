description: Turn any input or file into a tight, skimmable summary
---

Summarize the content I give you (or the attached file) so it can be skimmed in under 30 seconds.

- First, a single one-line takeaway.
- Then 3-6 short bullet points of the key facts, in order of importance.
- If there are numbers, dates, names, or decisions, keep them exact.
- Note anything ambiguous or worth following up on, at the end.

Do not pad it. No throat-clearing intro, no "in conclusion".
